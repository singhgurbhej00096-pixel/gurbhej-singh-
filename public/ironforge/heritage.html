How it works

From attic to archive

The same four steps protect buyers and sellers on every Historic & Prestige sale.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Sellers describe the piece, its provenance and condition, and upload photographs.

  2. 02

    Authenticate

    Our Heritage Desk and an independent specialist examine the piece and verify lawful title.

  3. 03

    List with COA

    Approved pieces go live with a numbered certificate and a full condition report.

  4. 04

    Insured handover

    Funds sit in escrow while a fine-art courier delivers. Buyers get a 14-day review.

We accept

  • Antique arms & armour (pre-1900), sold as collectibles
  • Scientific & navigational instruments, timepieces
  • Medals, insignia and documented service groups
  • Maps, manuscripts and campaign furniture

We don't accept

  • Firearms or ammunition of any age
  • Items without lawful title or with unclear export status
  • Objects from active conflict zones or looted contexts
  • Replicas presented as originals
